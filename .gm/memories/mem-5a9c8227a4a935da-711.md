Runner 0.1.323 (c732127) does not fix the daemon-guard zombie wedge. At boot the daemon is the parent of the guard (launchd reaps it), so crash 1 recovers in 1-2 s and the guard is then the parent; crash 2 leaves a zombie (ps stat Z), every new daemon logs lost the atomic ownership claim -- pid already owns the shared daemon, and gm stays down past 100 s under SIGKILL or SIGTERM. 0.1.211 wedges the same way. The agentplug PR 1 build (f5d5f83) replaced the daemon 4 of 4 times in 1-2 s (daemon child exited and was reaped -- restarting on this poll, restart marker stamped), so its fixes are not redundant.
