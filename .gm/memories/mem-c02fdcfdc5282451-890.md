Building rs-plugkit wasm on this Mac: `nice` is SIP-protected and strips DYLD_*, so rust-lld dies at link with SIGABRT "Library not loaded: @rpath/libLLVM.dylib" (toolchain stable-aarch64-apple-darwin 1.98.1). Run `nice -n 10 env PATH=<toolchain>/bin DYLD_FALLBACK_LIBRARY_PATH=<toolchain>/lib CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=3 cargo build -p rs-plugkit --release --target wasm32-wasip1 --features slim --offline`. A scratch clone made with `cp -cR` (APFS clonefile keeps mtimes and target/) plus the submodule gitdir copied into .git rebuilds in 30-60 s; the clone needs core.worktree removed from .git/config. Verify a wasm change with AGENTPLUG_NO_DAEMON=1 AGENTPLUG_HOME=<scratch home holding plugins/*.wasm copies> ~/.gm-tools/agentplug-runner dispatch gm <verb> <json> (plain-text verbs take the raw code), never against the live daemon. Verified 2026-10-13, rs-plugkit PR AnEntrypoint/rs-plugkit#1.
